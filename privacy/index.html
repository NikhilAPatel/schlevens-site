Privacy policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

The short version

Schlevens does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

The app saves your settings, your odds and your roll log on your device so they're still there next time. None of it is sent to us or anyone else.

You can erase the roll log with Clear log in the app. Deleting the app removes the rest, though your device's own backups may keep a copy until they're replaced.

Shake to roll

Shake to roll uses the system's shake gesture. The app doesn't read or store motion data.

Apple

Apple runs the App Store and may collect information under its own privacy policy. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't identify you.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it doesn't collect information at all.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, see the support page.
